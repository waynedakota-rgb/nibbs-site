Child safety standards

Nibbs: Homework Planner is made by Vectrix LLC for students 13 and older in the United States. We don't allow anyone under 13.

What we don't allow

We do not allow child sexual abuse or exploitation in Nibbs. That includes content, names or behavior that sexualizes a minor, grooming, and any attempt to move a teen into private contact.

How Nibbs is built to keep teens safer

  • Trees are split by age. Teens (13–17) can only join Trees with other teens. Adults (18+) can only join Trees with adults.
  • No messages or chat. Trees members can only send preset cheers and reactions, and share assignments they already have.
  • Sharing starts off. A teen's schedule is shared with no one until they choose to share it.

Reporting

Anyone can report or block a Trees member in the app. We review reports promptly, serious ones first. We remove content that breaks these rules and remove people who post it, up to closing their account.

Contact

To report a child safety concern, email nibbs-support@googlegroups.com, or in the app open Help, then Contact support.
