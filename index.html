Nibbs: Homework Planner

Nibbs plans your homework one bite at a time.

Add your assignments and tests. Nibbs ranks them by how much they matter and how long they take, then fits them into the homework times you choose. Big jobs get split into bites across days, so nothing piles up the night before it's due.

What it does

  • Ranks your work and builds today's plan from the time you actually have.
  • Splits big assignments and test study across the days before they're due.
  • A focus timer with breaks, and a plan that adjusts when you fall behind.
  • Trees: small study groups with friends your own age. Share an assignment, cheer each other on, and choose who can see your schedule.
  • Works offline. Sign in with Google to sync between devices.

Who it's for

Students 13 and older in the United States. Nibbs is not for children under 13.
