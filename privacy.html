Privacy policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

Nibbs: Homework Planner ("Nibbs") is made by Vectrix LLC ("we", "us"). Nibbs helps students plan their homework one bite at a time. Nibbs is made for teens and college students: high school and college students aged 13 and older in the United States. We don't let anyone under 13 use it.

This policy explains what Nibbs collects, why, who sees it, and how you delete it. It covers the Nibbs app on Android, iPhone and the web. If anything is unclear, ask us in the app under Help, Contact support, or email nibbs-support@googlegroups.com.

We only collect what Nibbs needs to plan your homework and keep your account working.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or browsing history. We do not use your camera.

On the web, Nibbs doesn't set cookies. The web app keeps your sign-in and settings in your browser's own storage, and your homework in a private database inside your browser. When you sign in with Google, Google may set its own cookies on its own site. We don't read them.

How we use it

  • Rank and schedule your homework, and keep your plan in sync across your devices.
  • Send the notifications you turned on (today's plan, deadline risk, break's over).
  • Let Trees members see what you chose to share, and keep teens and adults in separate Trees.
  • Answer your support tickets and fix bugs you report.
  • Keep Nibbs safe: act on reports, enforce blocks, and stop abuse.

What we never do:

  • We don't build advertising profiles of you ourselves. If you're 18 or older and allow personalized ads, Google AdMob can use device information to pick ads for you. Some state privacy laws call that a "sale" or "sharing". You can say no at any time (see Your choices). Teens never get personalized ads.
  • We don't read your homework to train AI models.
  • We don't use outside analytics or tracking companies. Google AdMob, which shows our ads, is the only outside ad company in Nibbs.

Students aged 13 to 17

  • Trees are split by age. Teens (13–17) can only join Trees with other teens. Adults (18+) can only join Trees with adults.
  • Sharing starts off. A teen's schedule is shared with no one until they change it, and they can switch it back at any time.
  • No free typing between users. Trees members can only send preset cheers and reactions. Sharing an assignment copies a task you already have, so there's no message box. Anyone can report or block another member.
  • We store an age group, not a birthday. The first time you open Nibbs, before you sign in, you pick your birth month and year. We work out "13–17" or "18+", save only that, and discard the rest. You can set it once. If it's wrong, ask support.
  • Under 13 means no Trees. If the month and year you enter show you're under 13, Trees stays closed for that account, even if you try again. If it was a typo, ask support.
  • Your app store's age check. On some phones, Google Play or Apple tells Nibbs an age range for your account (for example, "13 to 15") and whether a parent has approved the app. This depends on where you live and your store settings. Nibbs only uses it to be more careful: under 13 locks Nibbs on that phone and closes Trees, a parent who hasn't approved yet sees a "ask a parent" screen, and a teen range keeps the account in the 13–17 group. It never moves an account into 18+. We save only the age group and which store it came from, not the range.
  • No personalized ads for teens, ever. Teens who say yes to ads get non-personalized ads only.

Parents and guardians can ask us to see or delete their teen's data by emailing nibbs-support@googlegroups.com. We may ask the teen to confirm the request from their own account first.

Children under 13

Nibbs is made for teens and college students. It isn't for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If the age you enter shows you're under 13, we don't save an age group and no ads load. If we learn that a child under 13 gave us personal information, we delete it. Parents and guardians can tell us at nibbs-support@googlegroups.com.

Your choices

  • Delete your account. In Nibbs, go to Settings, Account, Delete my account. This deletes your account and everything in it, and any Trees you planted. It can't be undone. You can also ask us by email. See Delete your account.
  • What deleting doesn't reach. Copies of your support tickets in our private support tracker stay until we remove them; ask and we will. Homework saved on another phone or browser stays there until you sign out on it. App settings stay on the device until you uninstall Nibbs or clear your browser data. Assignments someone already accepted from you stay in their plan, because they're now their copy.
  • See or get a copy of your data. Make "My school year (PDF)" in Settings for your homework history, or ask us for everything we hold about you.
  • Fix your data. Edit anything you typed in the app. For your age group, ask support.
  • Control sharing. In Trees, Manage sharing sets who sees your schedule: No access, Free/busy, or Full. You can leave a Tree or block anyone. Blocking someone ends their access to your schedule and clears anything they sent you that's still waiting. Access also ends when you no longer share a Tree.
  • Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information. Adults (18+): in Nibbs, go to Settings, Ads privacy, Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information. This turns off personalized ads, and you'll still see non-personalized ads. You can change it at any time. You can also ask us by email.
  • Turn off notifications in Settings, Notifications, or in your phone's settings.

We answer requests within 30 days. We won't treat you differently for using any of these rights.

Keeping, protecting and changing this policy

  • How long we keep it. As long as your account is open. When you delete your account, it's removed from our database right away.
  • Security. Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules let each person read only their own data, and Trees data only for Trees they belong to. No system is perfect. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we'll tell you.
  • Changes. If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and tell you in the app before a big change takes effect.
  • Contact. In the app: Help, Contact support. By email: nibbs-support@googlegroups.com.
